Airplane Mode

Airplane Mode

How it works: Turning this on flips a master kill-switch that instantly shuts down all wireless transmitters (Cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and GPS).

When to use it: To comply with airline regulations, save battery when you have no signal, or force a full network refresh.

Pro Tip: You can manually turn Wi-Fi and Bluetooth back on while Airplane Mode is active.
